Singapore General Hospital & Changi General Hospital

These two EDs do not currently publish a live patient count or wait-time figure on their websites. Each site gives general triage guidance (patients are seen by severity, not arrival order) and directs non-urgent cases to the NurseFirst Helpline (6262 6262) or a GP/polyclinic instead. During surges, hospitals have posted ad-hoc advisories on Facebook rather than updating a numeric widget.

Ministry of Health

Cluster-wide reported statistics, not real-time
MOH — Waiting Time for Admission to Ward

MOH periodically publishes average ED-to-ward admission waiting times across AH, CGH, KTPH, NUH (adult), NTFGH, SKH, SGH and TTSH. KKH and NUH Children's Emergency are excluded. This is a periodic report, not a live feed.

MOH statistics page ↗
How to read this: "Waiting" is the number of patients ahead of a new arrival. "To see doctor" is the estimated time until first medical consultation — not total time in the ED. "Bed wait" (NUHS cluster only) is the estimated time from decision-to-admit to an actual ward bed, which is often the longest part of a visit. Life-threatening cases are always seen immediately regardless of these figures, at every hospital.

Non-emergency conditions: call the NurseFirst Helpline on 6262 6262, or book a same-day GP/polyclinic slot at book.health.gov.sg, rather than visiting an ED.
